## Two kinds of data, two roles

1. **Your account** (you, our customer): we decide how this data is used, so we are its **controller**.
2. **Form submissions** (what your visitors type into your forms): you decide what to collect and why, so
   you are the controller and we are your **processor**. We handle submissions only to deliver the service,
   under the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa). If you sent a form on someone else's website, contact that
   website: they decide what happens to your message. We will pass your request on if you write to us.

We use only cookies that are needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on
the dashboard. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it.

## Form submissions

What is stored is decided by the form you build. For each submission we store the fields and files sent, the
site it came from, and its spam score. **The visitor's IP address is not stored**; it is held in memory for
10 minutes for rate limiting. Content is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Spam is deleted after 30 days;
other submissions are kept until the customer deletes them or for the period they choose. See
[Security](/security).

## Who else handles the data

Only the subprocessors listed in Annex 3 of the [DPA](/legal/dpa) (hosting, email delivery, payments), each
under a contract that protects the data. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.
